MoneyMaker is a companion app for app developers: it shows the revenue of their own apps, collected by the MoneyMaker service.

HOW TO REVIEW (no account needed): after the welcome screens, tap "Try the demo". The same button is on the sign-in screen. It opens a read-only demo business ("Focus Timer") with sample data: revenue, MRR, sales feed, world map, leaderboard and settings. Nothing to install and no link to open.

SIGN IN: real users can use Sign in with Apple, Google or email from the "I already have an account" button.

MONEYMAKER PRO (in-app purchase "io.moneymaker.pro.lifetime", non-consumable): a one-time lifetime license that unlocks unlimited businesses; the free plan includes 1 business. It is sold only through Apple In-App Purchase. To test it: gear icon (Settings), "Unlimited businesses", then the App Store purchase sheet (sandbox). "Restore" is on the same screen. The demo is read-only, so nothing is saved there.

ACCOUNT DELETION: Settings, "Delete my account" (real accounts; the demo is read-only).

LEADERBOARD: a business appears publicly only if its owner opts in. Each public business page lets users report or hide the business. Reports are reviewed manually; contact: contact@moneymakerr.com.

Notifications are optional and only report the developer's own sales. Tax and net figures are labeled as estimates, not financial advice.

The app supports iPad and iPhone Duo with adaptive layouts.
