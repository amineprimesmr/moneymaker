MoneyMaker is a free companion app for app developers: it shows the revenue of their own apps, collected by the MoneyMaker service (usemoneymaker.com).

EVERYTHING IS FREE DURING LAUNCH: there are no in-app purchases to test and nothing to buy. Settings shows "Full access — Free" (unlimited businesses, all features).

HOW TO REVIEW (no account needed): after the welcome screens, tap "Try the demo". The same button is on the sign-in screen ("I already have an account"). It opens a read-only demo business ("Focus Timer") with sample data: revenue, MRR, sales feed, world map, leaderboard and settings. Nothing to install and no link to open.

SIGN IN: real users can use Sign in with Apple, Google or email from the "I already have an account" button.

ACCOUNT DELETION: Settings, "Delete my account" (real accounts; the demo is read-only).

FEEDBACK: users can send an idea, a bug or a remark from the menu or Settings ("Share feedback"). It is linked to their account and only used to improve the app.

LEADERBOARD: a business appears publicly only if its owner opts in. Each public business page lets users report or hide the business. Reports are reviewed manually.

CONTACT: support@usemoneymaker.com

Notifications are optional and only report the developer's own sales. Tax and net figures are labeled as estimates, not financial advice.

The app supports iPad and iPhone Duo with adaptive layouts.
