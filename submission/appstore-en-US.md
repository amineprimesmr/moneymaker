## Description

MoneyMaker brings the revenue from all your apps and digital products (App Store and Stripe) into one real-time dashboard.

LIVE
• A "cha-ching" notification for every sale, trial, and refund
• Today's revenue on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island (Live Activity)
• Widgets: today's revenue, live sales, MRR with a goal, dashboard, rankings

YOUR NUMBERS
• MRR, revenue by period, paying users, downloads, trials, churn, conversion
• All your businesses together, or only the ones you pick
• The sales globe: see where your money comes from, country by country

WHAT YOU ACTUALLY KEEP (optional)
• From gross to take-home: VAT, App Store commission, Stripe fees, contributions, and taxes based on your legal status (French micro-entreprise, SASU, EURL, US LLC, UAE free zone, or your own rates)

APP STORE
• Rankings in 177 countries, with alerts when your app climbs or enters a new country

LEADERBOARD
• Compare your verified revenue with other businesses, if you choose to appear

FREE TO START
• The free plan tracks one business
• MoneyMaker Pro unlocks unlimited businesses, charts, ratings, and app health with a one-time purchase, no subscription

Create your account right in the app, then connect your revenue sources in minutes. Tax estimates are for guidance only and don't replace an accountant.
